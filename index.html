Our Services

Gatcombe Industrial Limited provide industrial roofing and cladding services across Portsmouth, Hampshire and the South of England — offering supply & fit and sub-contract solutions for projects of all sizes.

Metal Roof Sheeting

Profiled steel and coated metal sheet roofing for industrial and commercial buildings. We carry out full re-sheeting of existing roofs and new build installations using quality coated steel profiles built to withstand harsh weather.

Composite Roof Panels

Insulated composite panel systems including twin-skin and single-skin options for warehouses, factories and industrial units. Excellent thermal performance with fast installation for new build and refurbishment.

Flat Roofing Systems

EPDM rubber membrane, bituminous membrane and liquid waterproofing for low-pitch and flat industrial roofs. Durable, low-maintenance solutions backed by long-term manufacturer warranties.

Rainscreen Cladding

Ventilated rainscreen facade systems providing a weather-resistant outer skin, moisture control and superior thermal performance. Available in aluminium, steel and composite panel finishes.

Wall Cladding

Profiled metal and composite wall cladding for industrial and commercial buildings. Available in a wide range of colours, coatings and finishes combining durability with a clean appearance.

Standing Seam Roofing

Interlocking vertical panel standing seam systems offering a premium, modern finish. A popular choice on new build and high-specification refurbishment projects for their sleek aesthetic and long-term performance.

Over-Cladding & Re-Roofing

Cost-effective over-sheeting and over-cladding including asbestos cement replacement. Improve energy efficiency and weatherproofing with minimal disruption to your operations.

Rooflights & Accessories

Supply and installation of GRP and polycarbonate rooflights, gutters, fascias, soffits and flashings. All colour-matched to your roofing and cladding system.

Sub-Contract Services

Experienced roofing and cladding sub-contractor available to principal contractors and developers on larger commercial and industrial projects across the South of England and beyond.
